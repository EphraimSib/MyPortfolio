Musixvoice // AI music player

A music player with built-in AI that learns what you listen to and discovers your genre by your music taste, then recommends where to go next.

  • Plays all the music on your phone offline and streams online
  • Queue, playlists, favorites, albums and artists
  • Lock-screen and notification controls with background playback
  • AI taste profile, genre discovery and mood mixes
  • 7 futuristic themes, visualizer and equalizer

Smith Agent // multi-model AI agent

An agent that combines DeepSeek, Claude and Gemini. Each model works on your question from its own angle, then they compare notes to reach one realistic answer.

  • Three models working on one task
  • Cross-checking to catch weak assumptions
  • One merged, practical answer
  • Helps improve the other two projects

HOW A QUESTION FLOWS

1 · DeepSeekLogic, code and failure points
2 · ClaudeReasoning, trade-offs, clarity
3 · GeminiSynthesis and next steps
4 · ConsensusOne realistic answer
